# Change contract, cycle 4: L1 -> L2, job positions (what the L3 projection review found)

- **Prior policy authority:** `sketches/l1-brokerage-intent-v1.md`, the clauses listed for `job:positions`. Unchanged. The cycle-3 model as selected (`selected-model.json`) stands.
- **Exact active change authority:** `chain/l3/duckdb-native/positions/review-1.md`, the projection review of your model's first compile. It found that the model states some rules only in group and hole prose, which an L3 manifest cannot cite and an audit cannot check, and that two derivations have no invariant, so the mutation harness never exercises them.
- **Authorized additions (deterministic invariants, disposition candidate, each with derived_from a subset of its group's parent clauses, necessity, parallel_assumption, review_trigger, owner_hat; add each to the `feedback` of the attributes and handoffs it checks):**
  1. `inv.holding_quantity_change_is_difference` (entity logical.holding_change): on every row, quantity_change equals after_qty minus before_qty. Restates `logical.holding_change.quantity_change`'s derivation. Group sg.holding-shape.
  2. `inv.account_position_is_sum_of_changes` (logical.account_position) and `inv.customer_position_is_sum_of_changes` (logical.customer_position): each position row's net_quantity equals the sum of quantity_change over the holding_change rows carrying that row's key, and every key that appears among holding changes has exactly one position row. Restates the two sum_of_deltas handoffs. Group sg.no-phantom-positions.
  3. `inv.holding_change_ownership_present` (logical.holding_change): every persisted holding change carries all four ownership attributes non-null, because a change whose current trade's ownership pin does not resolve is excluded from this entity rather than carried with gaps. This lifts the rule already stated in `sg.holding-attribution`'s coverage_claim into an element an audit can check. Group sg.holding-attribution.
  4. `inv.holding_change_current_trade_known` (logical.holding_change): every raw.holding_history report whose cdc_flag is not D names an hh_t_id that resolves to a logical.trade row. This mechanizes the review trigger already on the `hh_t_id -> current_trade_number` attribute and on the trade ownership handoffs ("does not resolve to any logical.trade row"): a breach fires as an audit violation instead of vanishing from the sums. Group sg.holding-attribution. Its parallel_assumption is the handoff's own (hh_t_id names a real trade). Do not decide what the business would do with such a report; the invariant only makes the case visible.
  5. `inv.holding_quantities_present` (logical.holding_change): on every persisted holding change, before_qty, after_qty and quantity_change are non-null, because a report with no determinable before or after quantity is the review trigger already recorded on `type.holding_quantity` and both quantity attributes, and a sum over partly null members would silently drop them. Group sg.holding-shape.
